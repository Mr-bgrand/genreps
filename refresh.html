Refresh the collection.

Refreshes are controlled through your Vercel account. Visitors can browse the gallery; only project members can publish an update.

  1. Open Vercel and select watch-collection.
  2. Open Deployments, then the latest successful production deployment.
  3. Choose Redeploy from the menu and confirm the production environment.

Open Vercel to refresh ↗

A rebuild carries forward the previous archive, checks both public Dropbox folders, imports all active-watch photos, archives missing watches with one cover each, and replaces the gallery when the entire update succeeds. Allow a few minutes. Reload the gallery after the deployment is Ready.

Your current gallery stays available if a refresh fails. Existing cover choices are retained while their photos remain available. New watches use a suggested cover that may need review.
